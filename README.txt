Your blinds and awnings move when someone picks up the remote, and not otherwise. This app puts them in Homey, so the shade can come out when the sun reaches the terrace, go back in before the wind picks up, and close at night on its own.

Homey learns your own handset when you add a blind, so it only ever speaks to yours and never to a neighbour's. Each of the remote's five channels becomes its own device, and Homey keeps listening afterwards: when someone uses the handset by hand, Homey notices and stays in step instead of showing a stale position.
