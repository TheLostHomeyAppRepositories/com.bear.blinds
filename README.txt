Blinds and awnings tend to be the last thing still worked by a handset left on a windowsill, long after the lights and the heating have moved into Homey. This app brings them in, so the shade comes out when the sun reaches the terrace, goes back before the wind picks up, and closes for the night on its own.

Homey learns your own handset when you add a blind, so it only ever speaks to yours and never to a neighbour's. Each of the remote's five channels becomes its own device, and Homey keeps listening afterwards as well: when someone uses the handset by hand, Homey notices and stays in step rather than quietly holding a stale idea of where things are.
